# ADR-014 — Idempotency-Key with server-side dedup store

## Purpose
Constrain C-3's submission path to one idempotency mechanism before LLD.

## Summary (S1)
Client-supplied Idempotency-Key plus a server-side dedup store
(key → order_id, 24h TTL) inside Order Service. Rejected: natural-key dedup
(payload hash) and client-only dedup. C-3 is decided_by this record; if the
key contract or TTL changes, C-3 and its dependents must re-review.

## Scope & Non-scope
Engineering scope of FS-014/FS-020. Non-goals: cross-region dedup replication
this mission (accepted risk; NFS-011 covers single-region SLO).

## Context
FS-014 requires at-most-one order per retry; FS-020 requires the original
order id on retry. C-3 (Order Service) owns submission; retry windows on
mobile are minutes, dedup horizon is 24h.

## Options considered
1. Natural-key dedup (payload hash) — no client changes, but legitimate
   re-orders of the same basket get swallowed.
2. Client-only dedup — pushes correctness to untrusted clients.
3. Idempotency-Key + server-side dedup store — explicit intent, standard
   pattern, requires client contract change.

## Trade-off scoring
| option | correctness | client impact | complexity | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 6 |
| 2 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| 3 | 3 | 1 | 2 | 6 |

## Decision
Option 3 — Idempotency-Key header + dedup store (24h TTL) in C-3. Payload hash
is recorded as a secondary mismatch detector (same key, different payload ⇒
409).

## Consequences
Client contract change (assumption in FS-NFS); C-3 gains a store on the
submission path (NFS-011 risk); API-OP-09 must carry the header contract.
