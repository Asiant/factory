# KDD-002 — Partial-fulfilment policy (product)

## Purpose
Decide what "partial fulfilment" means for buyers and sellers before the HLD
hardens around one policy.

## Summary (S1)
Options: whole-order fulfilment only, line-granular partial fulfilment, or
shipment-batch granularity. Decision: line granularity — fulfil and track at
line level, cancellations apply to the unfulfilled remainder only. Seller
payouts stay whole-order in this mission (explicit non-goal).

## Scope & Non-scope
Product semantics of FS-015/017/018/019. Non-goals: settlement mechanics.

## Context
Buyers cancel whole orders today when only part shipped; sellers re-ship
duplicates. The mission's value hypothesis depends on partial state being
visible and actionable (FS-015 family).

## Options considered
1. Whole-order fulfilment only — simple, but status truth stays ambiguous.
2. Line-granular partial fulfilment — matches seller warehouse reality.
3. Shipment-batch granularity — extra concept buyers do not need.

## Trade-off scoring
| option | buyer clarity | seller fit | complexity | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| 2 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 8 |
| 3 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 5 |

## Decision
Option 2 — line-granular partial fulfilment, remainder-only cancellation.

## Consequences
FS-015/017/018/019 obligations as stated; order state machine gains
partially-fulfilled; settlement explicitly out of scope this mission.

PO (accountable), seller ops lead (consulted), lead architect (consulted).
